# JXR Constructors — Page-by-Page Content & Layout Plan

Built from: [site-copy.md](site-copy.md) (current live copy), [brand-notes.md](brand-notes.md) (palette/type direction from the logo), [photo-catalog.md](photo-catalog.md) (photo inventory).

Design system reference: navy/charcoal + warm off-white background + the logo's black/blue/white as fixed anchors, one muted accent used sparingly, confident serif or humanist sans headline type, structural/blueprint line motifs instead of flag iconography. See brand-notes.md for full rationale.

## 2. About Us

**Goal:** Make the founder story the spine of the page — it's the single most differentiating asset on the whole site and currently reads as one paragraph among several.

**Structure, top to bottom:**
1. **Founder narrative, large treatment:** "In 2012, U.S. Marine Corps combat veteran Jesus Ramirez founded JXR Constructors..." — give this its own section with a portrait if available (check photo folder for headshots; none identified in the catalog, flag as a gap). Include the UC San Diego structural engineering credential explicitly — that's a credibility detail buried in the current copy.
2. **Timeline strip:** 2012 (founded) → today, with 2-3 milestone markers (SDVOSB certification, first federal contract, $30M+ delivered). Even a simple 3-4 point horizontal timeline adds narrative weight the current page lacks.
3. **Credentials as badges, not prose:** SDVOSB / DVBE / CA Small Business — rendered as a small badge row (icon + label), pulling this out of the paragraph it's currently trapped in.
4. **Team section:** "70+ years combined experience" — keep, but pair with a photo if any team/jobsite photos exist outside named project clusters (check `IMG_0111-0132` stairwell/utility shots — likely not suitable; flag as another photo gap: no team photos currently identified).
5. **Values, redesigned:** "HONESTY. PROFESSIONALISM. PRIDE in our work." — currently three stacked words. Give each one a single supporting sentence tying it to something concrete already in the copy (e.g., Pride → zero safety violations; Honesty → transparent communication mission statement) so the values feel earned, not generic.
6. **Federal expertise callout:** VA, NAVFAC, Army Corps of Engineers, Federal Bureau of Prisons — keep as a credibility list.
7. Closing tagline: "Veteran-Owned. Mission-Driven." as a section-ending statement, not buried mid-page.

## 3. Our Services

**Goal:** Current page treats all four services as equally weighted list items. Split by buyer type, since federal/institutional and commercial clients are evaluating JXR on different criteria (compliance vs. cost/speed/disruption).

**Structure:**
1. **Intro** (keep, tighten): "end-to-end construction services with particular strength in occupied facility renovations, design-build execution, and high-compliance public contracts."
2. **Track A: Federal & Institutional** — Federal & Institutional Expertise + General Contracting content, emphasizing ICRA, ATFP, infection control, phased construction in occupied/secure environments. Explain acronyms briefly for lay readers (e.g., "ICRA (Infection Control Risk Assessment)").
3. **Track B: Commercial & Tenant Improvement** — Tenant Improvements + Design-Build content, emphasizing minimal disruption, ADA compliance, timeline/change-order reduction.
4. **Supporting capabilities strip:** New Construction, Renovations, Turnkey Construction, Civil Work/Landscaping, Project Management, Scheduling (P6 CPM), Estimating — currently listed only on Home; consolidate here as the definitive services list so Home doesn't duplicate it in full.
5. **Closing CTA section** (keep, tighten copy slightly — current closing paragraph is a bit salesy/generic compared to the rest of the site's tone): "Book An Appointment."
